Drafts and "never lose your place" navigation.

**Drafts.** Everything you write in compose is now saved as you type, until you post it or discard it. That includes the reply or quote target, every post of a thread, and attached images and video with their alt text. You can keep many drafts at once. Closing the composer (Esc, ×, a stray click on the backdrop, or quitting the app) never loses work. A **Resume** chip above the + button brings your latest draft back. Replying to a post you already started a reply to picks up where you left off. The composer header has a **Drafts** list to switch between drafts and **New** to start another. Your old single draft is imported automatically.

**Threads.** "+ Add post" chains posts into a thread, for new posts and replies alike. Each post gets its own character counter, up to 4 images, and clipboard paste. **Split into thread** breaks an over-long post at sentence and word boundaries. The whole thread is checked before anything is published. If a post fails partway through, the part that went out stays live and the rest is kept as a reply to it, so retrying finishes the thread instead of posting it twice.

**Getting back to where you were.**
- Threads and profiles have back/forward buttons (⌘[ / ⌘]).
- ⌘⇧T, or the **Reopen** toast, brings back a thread or profile you just closed, scrolled to where you left it.
- A new **History** panel (⌘Y, or the clock in the sidebar) lists recently viewed threads and profiles.

Fixes found along the way:
- A post opened from inside a profile now appears on top of it instead of hidden behind it.
- "Continue thread →" on deep replies now opens the reply instead of doing nothing.
- Video-only posts can be sent.
- Esc in the @mention list closes just the list, and Esc closes the composer before the sheet under it.
- A reply sent from an open thread now shows up in it.
