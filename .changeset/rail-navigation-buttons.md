Back, forward, reopen and History are now always-visible buttons at the top of the left sidebar, right under the logo. You no longer need the keyboard shortcuts or the buttons inside a thread. ← and → step through whichever thread or profile is open, ↺ brings back the one you just closed, and the clock opens History. While a thread or profile is open, the buttons stay clear and clickable on top of the dimmed background, right where you need a way back.
