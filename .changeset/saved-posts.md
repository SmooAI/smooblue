"smooblue": minor
---

Save posts to read later. Every post has a bookmark button next to Like; click it to save, and it fills orange. A new **Saved** column shows everything you've saved, newest first. Open it from the bookmark button in the sidebar or with `g b`. Smooblue uses Bluesky's own private bookmarks, so these are the same saves you see in the Bluesky app on your phone and on bsky.app. Save something anywhere and it shows up everywhere. Unsaving a post removes it from the Saved column right away.
